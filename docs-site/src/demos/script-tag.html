Local library extends opening hours

Starting next month, the central library will stay open until nine in the evening on weekdays. The change follows a survey in which most respondents said they could only visit after work. Weekend hours are unchanged.

Staff say the reading room has been the most requested space, especially among students preparing for exams, and that the longer hours should ease crowding in the afternoons.
