# Install Text Size Adjuster

Text Size Adjuster is a Firefox add-on. It works on your computer and on your Android phone.

> **Not available yet.** Text Size Adjuster isn't on Firefox Add-ons yet. Once it is, the steps
> below are all you'll need. Until then, technically confident users can use one of the
> [advanced installation options](../index.html#advanced).

## On your computer

1. In Firefox, open the Text Size Adjuster page on [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/)
   (link added once it's available).
2. Click **Add to Firefox**, then **Add** when Firefox asks.

## On your Android phone

1. Open Firefox, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Extensions** (or **Add-ons**).
2. Find **Text Size Adjuster** in the list or search, and tap **+**.
3. Tap **Add** when Firefox asks.

## How to use it

- On any page, a small round control appears in the bottom-right corner. Tap **+** to make the
  text bigger and **−** to make it smaller. **↺** goes back to normal, and **×** hides the control
  on that page.
- The text gets bigger *within* the page: it wraps to fit your screen, so you don't have to scroll
  sideways. Pictures and the page layout keep their size.
- Your chosen size is remembered for each website. Next time you visit, it's already applied.
- You can also adjust the size from the Text Size Adjuster button in Firefox's toolbar (or the
  extensions menu on Android).

Curious how this is different from your phone's own text-size setting? See
[how it differs](how-it-differs.html). Some things it can't change are listed under
[what it can't fix](limitations.html).
